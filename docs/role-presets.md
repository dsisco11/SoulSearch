# Role and combat presets

Role presets combine job skills with their relevant attribute priorities. They
contain Manager, Bookkeeper, Broker, Chief Medical Dwarf, Interrogator, Doctor,
and Animal Trainer.

Combat presets are listed separately in the preset picker. They include a
generic Soldier, every melee specialist (Axedwarf, Swordsdwarf, Knife User,
Macedwarf, Hammerdwarf, Speardwarf, Pikedwarf, Lasher, and Wrestler), plus
Marksdwarf, Hunter, and Hammerer. Each combat preset includes Dodging alongside
its main weapon and supporting military skills.

The role and combat mappings are based on the Dwarf Fortress Wiki's
[associated-attribute skill table](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Attribute#Skills_by_associated_attributes)
and [soldier skill list](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Soldiers).
